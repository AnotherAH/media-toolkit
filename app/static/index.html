Record a live stream

Works on any site the app supports — YouTube, Twitch, Kick, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, news channels, radio. Video and audio are recorded together into one file, copied as-is with no re-encoding, so there is no quality loss and almost no CPU use.

You can stop a recording at any time from the Queue and keep everything captured so far — the file is finalised properly, not left broken.

Queue

This machine

Transcription backend is picked automatically and verified before use.

Downloaded Whisper models

Models are downloaded once, on first use. If a download is interrupted the app detects the damage and re-fetches automatically — you can also force that here.

GPU acceleration

Transcribing on the GPU is many times faster than on the CPU. The libraries are a large one-time download, so they are fetched only if you want them.

Signing in to sites

Needed only for private, members-only or age-restricted content. There is no such thing as a fake session cookie — sites check it against a real login — but you never have to build a cookies.txt by hand.

Paste cookies manually

Accepts a whole cookies.txt, or a plain name=value; name=value cookie header copied from your browser's dev tools.
